6. FINDINGS
-----------------------------------------------------

The local network is healthy:
  ✅ Router responds quickly (10–33 ms)
  ✅ 3 devices connected successfully
  ✅ DNS resolution works
  ✅ Web requests succeed

The ISP connection is the bottleneck:
  ❌ Ping to Google averages 1414 ms (should be 20–100 ms)
  ❌ 50% packet loss — half the packets disappear
  ❌ This is far worse than the laptop's result (213–341 ms)

Root cause: Airtel Kenya's network has a serious routing
or congestion problem. This is confirmed by:
  - The laptop's traceroute showing slowness at hop 3–4
  - The phone's very high ping times
  - The 50% packet loss
